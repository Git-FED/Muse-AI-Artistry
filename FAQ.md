# FAQ

### Which page is the home page?
`index.html` remains Lumen Home. MuseAI promotion is a separate page at `museai.html`.

### Does this site need a build step?
No. It is static HTML/CSS/JavaScript and can be hosted on GitHub Pages.

### Why are payment options hidden?
The site asks visitors to confirm an age of at least 18 before it loads support payment widgets. The browser-only DOB check is not identity verification or a legal compliance guarantee.

### What does support cost?
This repository lists no prices. The providers display their configured terms before checkout; the owner should verify those settings before publishing.
