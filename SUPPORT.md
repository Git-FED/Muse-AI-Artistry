# Support

For website or project questions, email `support@fedpromptly.com`. For partnership inquiries use `business@fedpromptly.com`; careers inquiries can go to `careers@fedpromptly.com`; general contact is `contact@fedpromptly.com`.

Payment setup and amounts are managed by the linked provider accounts. The age gate on this website is a local, browser-only date check and is not identity verification.
