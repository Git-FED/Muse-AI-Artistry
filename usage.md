# Usage guide

Use the gallery navigation to switch between visual views. `index.html` is the Lumen Home spotlight gallery; choose **MuseAI** for the separate promotional page. The galleries keep their individual controls, such as scroll, drag, hover, focus, and arrow keys.

The support button opens payment options only after an 18+ date-of-birth check. The date is used only to calculate age in the current page; the page does not persist it or transmit it. Payment details and terms are displayed by the linked providers.
